## Status Values

- `candidate`: found and potentially useful, not yet evaluated.
- `approved`: suitable for thesis integration after cleaning and deduplication.
- `rejected`: not SMS-like, not English, no usable labels, no clear source, unsafe/private, or otherwise unsuitable.
- `needs_review`: labels, license, data quality, language, or source reliability are unclear.
- `already_used`: already included in the thesis, such as UCI, Mishra & Soni, or SmishTank if applicable.

## Notes

- `approved` means the dataset is suitable for integration after cleaning and deduplication.
- `needs_review` means the labels, license, or data quality are unclear.
- `rejected` means the source should not be imported.
- General spam should not be mapped to smishing unless the message clearly attempts phishing or fraud.
